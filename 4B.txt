Required materials are identified based on:

Project drawings
BOQ (Bill of Quantity)
Site conditions
Construction activities

Different activities require different materials.

Examples:

Foundation work → Cement, steel, aggregates
Brickwork → Bricks, cement, sand
Flooring → Tiles, granite, marble
Electrical work → Wires, switches, conduits

Proper material requirement planning helps avoid shortages, delays, and excess purchasing.
