Construction materials are all materials used during project execution to complete civil, structural, electrical, plumbing, and finishing activities.



Major construction materials:



* Cement
* Steel
* Bricks
* Sand
* Concrete
* Aggregates
* Wood
* Glass
* Paint
* Tiles



The quality of construction materials directly affects:



* Building strength
* Durability
* Safety
* Project lifespan
* Maintenance cost



Proper selection and quality checking of construction materials are essential for successful project completion.
