Construction materials are the basic resources used to build and complete a construction project. Materials play a major role in project strength, quality, durability, safety, and cost. The selection of proper materials depends on project type, design, environmental condition, budget, and construction standards.

Common construction materials include:

Cement
Sand
Steel rods
Bricks
Aggregates
Concrete
Tiles
Paint
Wood
Glass

Materials are mainly classified into:

Structural materials
Finishing materials
Electrical materials
Plumbing materials
Waterproofing materials

Proper material planning helps reduce wastage, improve quality, control project cost, and ensure smooth project execution.
