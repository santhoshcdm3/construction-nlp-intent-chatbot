Material classification is the process of grouping materials based on their usage and function in construction projects.



Main classifications:



* Structural Materials
1. Cement
2. Steel
3. Concrete
* Finishing Materials
1. Paint
2. Tiles
3. Glass
* Electrical Materials
1. Wires
2. Switches
3. Panels
* Plumbing Materials
1. Pipes
2. Valves
3. Water fittings



Material classification helps improve inventory management, stock monitoring, budgeting, and procurement planning.
